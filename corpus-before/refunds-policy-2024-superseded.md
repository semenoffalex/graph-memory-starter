# Refunds policy (2024)

> ⚠️ Superseded January 2026 — refunds are now covered by the Customer Operations Handbook. Kept for reference only.

Refund requests are logged in the shared sheet and processed weekly on Fridays.

Refunds over £250 require director approval. Refunds under £250 can be approved by any team member after checking the order history.

Store credit is offered first in all cases; cash refunds only where the customer insists or the law requires it.
